About me

I’m a customer-centric Product Manager that thinks in systems and learns through experimentation.

I came to product management through the sciences and customer-facing work. Along the way, I learned to question assumptions, test ideas, and pay close attention to what people actually need.

At Wonder Project, that led me to some pretty complex problems across the streaming experience—from content delivery and metadata to content management, privacy, and the DTC product. I like getting into the details, understanding how the pieces connect, and finding ways to make complicated systems easier to use.
