02 — Workflow Automation

Automating manual, high-touch steps of the content delivery workflow

Mapped the end-to-end delivery workflow, automated high-touch steps, and shipped the first roadmap feature under real budget and SLA constraints.

The problem

Delivery work was fragmented and high-touch. Content Operations spent meaningful time on manual data entry, manual generation of deliverables, and a decentralized workflow that didn’t scale with catalog volume.

The product opportunity

If the workflow could be modeled as software—with clear inputs, validation, and partner handoffs—ops time could move from repetitive production work back to judgment and exception handling.

My approach

  1. Customer needs

    Interviewed Content Operations stakeholders, mapped the end-to-end workflow, and identified bottlenecks: manual data entry, manual deliverable generation, and decentralized process ownership.

  2. Roadmap

    Created the roadmap and executive memo for a phased automation solution that delivered value incrementally while respecting budget constraints.

  3. Buy-in

    Used AI-assisted coding tools (Cursor) to put working prototypes in front of stakeholders—so feedback and alignment happened against something real, not a slide.

  4. Implementation

    Shipped the first roadmap feature: automated avails generation, validation, and delivery by connecting to free Amazon Content APIs.

  5. Evaluation

    The feature met and exceeded the success metric of 10 hours per week saved, while staying within guardrails of 99% SLA and <5% delta in rejections.

Outcome

50 hours/month returned to Content Operations.

The point wasn’t “we automated a file.” It was identifying an operational bottleneck, translating it into a product slice that could ship under budget, and proving leverage with explicit success and guardrail metrics.
