03 — DTC Streaming

Building a cohesive streaming experience across 7 platform ecosystems

Led development of the SVOD app to a feature-complete internal alpha—fully testable end to end, certification ready, with payment and playback across thousands of titles.

The problem

A coherent streaming product has to hold together across TV, mobile, and web—even when each platform has different constraints, certification paths, and interaction models. The work wasn’t a feature checklist; it was keeping one product coherent while shipping where each ecosystem demands.

Product problems I owned

Profiles

Edit profiles, add profiles, profile switching, and per-profile personalization—consistent across device classes.

Downloads

Offline download and playback, synced watch progress between downloads and streamed content, storage management, license expiration, and queueing.

Sign up

Plan selection, account creation, checkout, funnel analysis via GA, and Evergent identity & access management integration.

Legal & data privacy

Compliance with FTC, CCPA, and GDPR requirements across the website and consumer apps—including consent and preference surfaces.

Outcome

Reached a feature-complete internal alpha across seven device ecosystems: Roku, Apple TV, Fire TV, Google TV, iOS, Android, and Web. The build was fully testable end to end, certification ready, and capable of payment and playback across thousands of titles.
