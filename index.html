Media Supply Chain

Ingestion, QC, and Delivery Pipeline

Architected a manifest-driven ingestion, QC, and delivery pipeline from 0 to 1.

  • Enabled content operations to deliver 1500 titles for Prime Video Channels launch
  • 5,000 hours of content delivered as of May 2026 with 99% against Amazon SLA

Building 0 to 1, scaling to meet demand

Discovery
Assessed the core needs of a content delivery system for lean team planning for niche library catalog launch of 1500 titles — delivery spec, metadata schema, supply architecture.
Build vs Buy
Evaluated the tradeoffs of buying vendor tooling vs building custom. Decided on a hybrid solution leveraging Brightcove's transcoding, storage, and content management capabilities while keeping orchestration in-house.
MVP Launch
Shipped MVP, tested with internal teams and studio partners, fixed validation and ingestion bugs, re-wrote delivery spec, adopted by Content Operations.
Iteration
Extended supply chain from owned-endpoint to third-party distribution by conforming to Amazon's spec, automating MEC/MMC generation, integrating MediaInfo spec QC.
Workflow Automation

Automating Content Delivery Systems

Mapped the end to end delivery workflow and automated manual, high-touch steps.

  • Returned 50 hours per month to Content Operations.

Aligning stakeholders on new features under budget constraints

Customer Needs
Interviewed content operations stakeholders, mapped end to end workflow, and identified key areas of improvement: manual data entry, manual generation of deliverables, decentralized workflow.
Roadmap
Created the roadmap and executive memo for a phased automation solution that delivers value incrementally while keeping budget constraints in mind.
Buy-In
Leveraged AI-assisted coding tools like Cursor to get working prototypes in front of stakeholders to get feedback and buy-in on the solution.
Implementation
Shipped first feature on roadmap — automated avails generation, validation, and delivery — by connecting to free Amazon Content APIs.
Evaluation
Feature met and exceeded Success Metric of 10 hours per week saved while staying within Guardrail Metrics of 99% SLA and <5% delta in rejections.
